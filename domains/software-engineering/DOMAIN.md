# Domain pack: Empirical Software Engineering (incl. research software)

Computational studies of software, developers and software processes: mining software repositories
(MSR), testing research (differential, metamorphic, fuzzing), defect/effort prediction, code review,
CI/CD, dependency ecosystems, and the quality of **scientific/research software** (e.g. crop models,
climate models, bioinformatics tools), a strong bridge between software engineering and other domains.

## What makes an SE paper Q1
- A clear, general research question (not "we built a tool"), answered with an evaluation that would
  convince a skeptic: realistic subjects, strong baselines, statistical rigour, threats to validity.
- Replication package (code, data, scripts) archived with a DOI; many venues award artifact badges.
- Follows the **ACM SIGSOFT Empirical Standards** for the study type.

## Subfields
MSR & ecosystems · software testing (differential, metamorphic, mutation, fuzzing) · program analysis ·
AI for SE (LLMs for code) & SE for AI · defect prediction · performance · security (vulnerability
studies) · human factors (surveys, interviews: not computational) · research software engineering.
