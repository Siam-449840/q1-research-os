# Venue map (agriculture and adjacent)

> **Quartiles are NOT stated here on purpose.** They change every year and differ by subject category.
> Before submission the owner verifies quartile and indexing on SCImago (https://www.scimagojr.com/)
> and/or Clarivate JCR, and records it in `submission/JOURNAL_CHECK.csv` (`rosctl gate S9` requires it).
> Also check scope, recent articles, APC/subscription options and the AI-use policy.

## Choosing well

1. Read 3–5 papers the journal published in the last 2–3 years that resemble yours (method + scale).
2. Match article type and length; some journals favour applied studies, others methods or theory.
3. Check open-access options and fees; many hybrid journals have a no-fee subscription route.
4. Avoid predatory venues: verify indexing yourself, check "Think. Check. Submit."
   (https://thinkchecksubmit.org/), distrust unsolicited invitations and guaranteed fast acceptance.
